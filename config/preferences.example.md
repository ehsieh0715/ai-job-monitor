# Personal preferences

Copy to config/preferences.local.md (Git-ignored) and set preferences_path in
config.local.yaml to config/preferences.local.md, or use another private path.
Only record your own choices; this example is not an assertion about any candidate.

- Profile: concise summary with source-linked detail; preserve confirmed facts.
- Analysis language: Traditional Chinese.
- CV / cover letter language: professional English.
- CV length: adapt to target market and employer instructions.
- Writing: direct, specific, evidence-led; section order depends on the role.

You may change the Profile organization, emphasis, tone or output format. Personal
preferences do not change shared scoring gates or turn unverified claims into facts.
