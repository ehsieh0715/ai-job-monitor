# Application Email Writing

- Provide a subject and concise body naming the role, strongest relevant fit and attachments that exist or are being prepared; do not repeat the full letter.
- Use requested language; Ireland/UK default is professional Irish or British English. Use a neutral greeting for unknown contacts.
- Do not invent recipients, familiarity, motivation, achievements or sent attachments. Keep facts consistent with CV or letter.
- Save the email as Markdown under workflow path, naming and version rules unless conversation-only drafting was requested.
- Never send as a side effect of evaluation, drafting or export; sending requires explicit instruction.
