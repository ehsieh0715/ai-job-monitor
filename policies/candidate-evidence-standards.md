# Candidate Evidence Standards

Version: 1.5.

## Sources

- Resolve `career.root` from local config using `config/README.md`; use the fixed layout in `career-workflow.md`. Report exact missing paths or access; do not guess another person's sources.
- Read Candidate Profile first, including relevant projects. Verify material claims using originals in `Source Documents/` or its `Projects.md` links; incomplete Profile means review or questions, not confirmed absence.
- An explicitly designated file designated for review, revision or export may be read outside Source Documents. This allows no surrounding scan and does not make that file an authoritative Profile source.
- CV variants and repeated claims are presentations, not independent evidence. Clearer wording, length, order or keyword frequency cannot change capability credit; substantive evidence or corrected assessment can. Explain changes.
- Keep document-quality review separate from job-fit scoring.

## Claim boundaries

- Label evidence as direct or adjacent professional, portfolio or project, academic, learning or mentioned-only, or planned or in progress.
- **Employment record:** preserve employer, dates, contract or part-time status and underlying title.
- **Title clarification:** use a functional clarification only with supporting duties; do not invent promotions or replace contractual history.
- **Transferability:** a different historical title alone does not reduce credit for relevant work.
- Use only supported skills, actions, scope, metrics and outcomes. Separate candidate contributions from team/repository capabilities; code or repository ownership alone does not prove personal authorship.
- Completed academic work can demonstrate technical capability, but is not commercial tenure. Employment alone does not prove production use; tutorials, prototypes and named tools do not prove completed production ownership.
- Distinguish implemented functionality from current operation: paused deployment does not erase completed work, and completed code does not establish a live release.
- **Review record:** retain contributions, results, limits, sources, inspected files, commit and review date.
- **Inspection:** review relevant implementation, tests and configuration, not README alone. Mark claims not inspected.
- **Reuse:** keep unchanged reviewed evidence; never invent a checked commit or move repositories by default.

## Unknowns and questions

- Missing evidence is unknown, not confirmed absence. Give no hypothetical credit.
- Ask about decision-changing context, owned action, completion, scope or results and source, at every recommendation level. Avoid irrelevant biography and repeated questions.
- Follow the fit policy's early-stop exception: one narrow reassessment condition replaces routine questions; do not investigate secondary gaps after a decisive screen.
- In batches record uncertainty without guessing, asking blocking questions or treating unapproved claims as facts.

## Profile approval

- Propose source-linked additions, replacements and removals with exact target paths, context, limits and confirmation provenance. Display the proposed content in conversation, not an unapproved draft file.
- A build or update request authorizes inspection and proposal; factual answers alone do not approve unseen Markdown. Obtain explicit approval of the displayed content before initial or later Profile writes, including project sections.
- Apply only approved edits and preserve unrelated facts; do not ask again for unchanged approved content.
- Present newly discovered edits separately. Keep unresolved claims as questions.
- Reassess after approved saved changes and explain changed judgments. Report blocked access instead of claiming a write.
- This gate does not replace separately authorized CV, letter, tracker or pipeline actions. Unattended runs cannot update Profile. Filesystem permission is still required.

## Preferences and authorization

- Personal choices about language, tone and format belong in ignored `policies/preferences.md`, not shared policy. Without it, follow current instructions and audience; do not assume this user's style for others.
- Record reusable corrections only when requested or covered by explicit standing permission. Merge by topic, replace superseded choices, avoid raw chat logs; keep one-job instructions local unless reusable intent is clear.
- Preferences may record explicit standing authorization, not infer new permission. They cannot change factual and scoring boundaries or Profile approval. Never save credentials, guessed preferences or planned achievements as facts.
